# Tape-reading loop

`tape-reading-loop.mp3` is assembled from two user-supplied Pixabay sound effects:

- [Cassette tape hiss](https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/technology-cassette-tape-hiss-26079/): 0.2-17.5 seconds.
- [Sony Cassette Recorder, button presses, hiss](https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/film-special-effects-sony-cassette-recorder-button-presses-hiss-50528/): 1.2-25.3 seconds. The rest of this recording, including its ending voice, is excluded.

The sections play in that order and repeat as one 41-second file. The button recording has compressed peaks and both joins are faded. Both source pages identify the sounds as available under the Pixabay Content License.
